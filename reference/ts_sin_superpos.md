# Superimposed sine waves of different frequencies and different amplitudes

Assuming a sample rate of 44100 Hz, the signal consists of two
consecutive parts of 3000 samples each: superimposed sine waves of 440
Hz, 880 Hz and 100 Hz in the first part, and of 500 Hz, 1200 Hz and 50
Hz in the second part.

## Usage

``` r
ts_sin_superpos
```

## Format

A numeric vector of length 6000 containing a time series signal.
